## Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to support this notebook. Eligible article pages have clearly labelled advertising spaces, separated from the writing and navigation. Ads depend on Google's approval, inventory and your privacy choices; an empty space is not a recommendation or endorsement.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, local storage and other identifiers to deliver, measure and personalise ads, prevent fraud and limit repeated ads. Requests can include your IP address, device and browser information, the page address and referring page. Google may process this information outside your country. See [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

Google's consent message is configured for visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland. It offers consent, do-not-consent and manage-options choices. Where the message applies, use **Google privacy choices** in the footer to revisit your decision and the listed advertising partners and purposes. Non-personalised or limited ads may still be possible under Google's settings. To stop this blog requesting ads in this browser, choose **Read without ads** above. This does not erase information already processed by Google or its partners; browser controls can remove existing cookies.

## Reading preferences and search

The light/dark reading preference is saved in your browser's local storage. Archive and global search run against this site's article index; there is no search account or search form submitted to a separate service. Search terms can appear in the page URL when you use archive filters. Do not put private information into a public search URL you share.

## Hosting, fonts and external links

GitHub Pages hosts the site and receives ordinary request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Google Fonts receives requests when your browser downloads the typefaces. Some historical posts contain third-party demos or embedded media, which can contact their providers when loaded. External websites and services have their own privacy practices.
